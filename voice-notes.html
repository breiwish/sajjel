Welcome to Sajjel

Record audio and take synchronized notes. Your text is timestamped automatically, so you can jump back to any moment later.

Record and Tag

Press Record, then type notes. Wrap any text in #hash marks# to create a tag. Press Ctrl+M or the pin button to drop a quick unnamed marker.

Replay Your Session

Load a recording and press Play. Toggle Live Text to watch your notes replay in sync with the audio — see exactly what you typed at each moment.

Navigate and Refine

Click any tag to jump to that moment. Right-click a tag to nudge its timestamp. Use Arrow keys to scrub (hold Shift for 30s jumps).

Everything is Saved

Notes auto-save to disk as you type. Use the Load button to browse previous sessions. Click the ? button anytime to see this guide again.
